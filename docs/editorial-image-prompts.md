These seven final assets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. They are pre-launch concept photography; the existing HarSun logo remains a separate, unchanged asset.

## Hero — `hero-chocolate.jpg`

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: HarSun Chocolates pre-launch website hero photograph, wide landscape composition for a dark premium website. Primary request: original editorial product photograph of a small assortment of beautifully tempered artisan chocolate bars, one snapped to show glossy interior, a few cocoa nibs and whole almonds, arranged on deep espresso stone with a folded blush-pink paper sleeve and a slender antique-gold ribbon. No brand names or readable print on the sleeve. Style: photorealistic luxury food campaign photography, believable tactile chocolate, refined and understated, contemporary Indian boutique confectionery mood. Composition: horizontal 3:2, the product grouping centered and slightly right with generous dark negative space near edges for responsive crop; no human subjects. Lighting: warm directional window light, soft shadows, rich highlights. Palette: espresso brown, cocoa black, dusty blush, warm cream, muted gold. Constraints: no logo, no text, no watermark, no floating chocolate, no exaggerated CGI shine, no unrelated props.
